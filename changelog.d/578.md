- **Colour harmonies**: in the colour box, choose the painters' wheel (red, yellow, blue, the default) or the screen's wheel, then a harmony (complementary, split complementary, analogous, triad, rectangle, square, shades): the search colours follow the current colour, and dragging a dot on the wheel turns the whole harmony. The box also has its tolerance slider and the number of photos found, a hierarchical search widens the tolerance rank by rank, "Search these colours" under a photo's palette starts from its main colours, and the box works from the keyboard (1–5, arrows, h, m, w, p, +/-, Enter). Settings: `[gui.colour_search] wheel` and `key_*`, `[palette] colour_widen`.
