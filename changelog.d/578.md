- **Colour harmonies**: in the colour box, choose the painters' wheel (red, yellow, blue, the default) or the screen's wheel, then a harmony (complementary, split complementary, analogous, triad, rectangle, square, shades): the search colours follow the current colour, and dragging a dot on the wheel turns the whole harmony. The box also has its tolerance slider and the number of photos found, a hierarchical search widens the tolerance rank by rank, "Search for these colours" (the binoculars next to the colours' source) starts from the photo's main colours, and the box works from the keyboard (1–5, arrows or hjkl, a, m, w, p, +/-, Enter). Settings: `[gui.colour_search] wheel` and `key_*`, `[colours] colour_widen`.
