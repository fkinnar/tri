- **All photos**: a virtual folder at the top of the folders panel gathers the photos of every folder already opened or computed, and its filters search them all (similar palettes, contains this colour). It opens at once from the search index, then completes in the background; Escape returns to the folder you came from. "Search everywhere" actions open it with the palette or colour filter already set, and the similar-palettes filter now shows its reference photo, with "Use this one".
