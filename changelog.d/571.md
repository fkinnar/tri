- **All photos**: a virtual folder at the top of the folders panel gathers the photos of every folder already opened or computed, and its filters search them all. It opens at once from the search index, then completes in the background; `g a` goes there and back (Escape returns too), and filters stay set, so a filter set in a folder then searches everywhere.
- **Similar** filter (`f m`): photos that look like a reference photo, as stacks do. The reference is shared with similar palettes (`f l`): taken from the highlighted photo, shown at the right of both filters, kept until removed (click its thumbnail) or replaced (`f t`, or the right-click box). Contains this colour is now `f o`, and the `f` menu says "Sharp" like the panel.
