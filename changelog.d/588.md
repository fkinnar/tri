- **Added**: a "masses" mode for the large colours and the image in the chosen step (`<leader> c m`, or the "Masses" checkbox): small patches take the colour of the mass around them and edges are smoothed, for a clear map of the photo when composing. Two sliders set the minimum patch size and the smoothing (`[gui.colours_view] masses_*`).
- **Added**: a "Lightness" slider in the masses mode weighs lightness against hue when splitting the image into colours, for a value study (`[colours] posterize_lightness` without the mode); and the split now keeps the better of two starts, so light areas no longer stay stuck with a dark background. Moving the slider changes where the masses are, never their colours.
