- **Folder analyses can be turned down** (settings, *Folder analyses*): exposure, sharpness, stacks and palettes each run in the background, on demand (nothing in the background; the tool stays greyed until the folder is computed with right-click › Compute) or removed (the tool leaves the bars, the panel, the menus and the help). The old `[scheduler] exposure`, `[sharpness] background` and `[similarity] background` keys still work.
