- **Added**: offloading a card of several sessions (#507): the dialog lists one row per shooting day, each checked session goes into its own folder, and copying, history and deleting from the card only touch those images. `[offload] day_cut` (4 o'clock) keeps a late evening in its session. Command line: `tri --offload <card> --session <day>`, `--dry-run` lists the sessions.
