- **Added**: offloading a card of several sessions (#507): the dialog lists one row per shooting day, each checked session goes into its own folder, and copying, history and deleting from the card only touch those images. "Choose the photos…" opens a gallery of the session (embedded previews, adjustable size, a check on each photo, the grid's gestures: Cmd/Ctrl+click, Shift+click, `v`, Space, Cmd/Ctrl+A, large view on double-click). `[offload] day_cut` (4 o'clock) keeps a late evening in its session. Command line: `tri --offload <card> --session <day>`, `--dry-run` lists the sessions.
