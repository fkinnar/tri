- **Large colours**: a box shows the highlighted photo as is, in its average colour (the dominant), or in 2 to 8 colours, to see the balance of colour masses; false colours make close colours easy to tell apart. Open it with `g c` or from the Colours section; `<leader> c 0` to `8` picks the number of colours, `<leader> c p` the perceived colours, for the panel's palette too. From the command line: `tri <folder> --colours --posterize K --to <folder>`. An experimental "Perceived colours" switch shows the colours as the eye sees them, adapted to the surrounding light (`--perceived` on the command line). Settings: `[gui.colours_view]`, `[colours] perceived_*`.
- **Changed**: the large histogram opens with `g i` (was `Shift+I`), like `g c` for the large colours.
- **Changed**: digit shortcuts (ratings, `f 3`, `c 3`…) answer the typed character, wherever it comes from (top row with or without Shift depending on the keyboard, numeric keypad): "Shift+3" only held on AZERTY. A `Shift+3` kept in `[keys]` stays the key.
