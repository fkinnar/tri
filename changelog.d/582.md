- **Large palette**: a box shows the highlighted photo as is, in its average colour (the dominant), or in 2 to 8 colours, to see the balance of colour masses; false colours make close colours easy to tell apart. Open it from the Palette section ("Large palette"). From the command line: `tri <folder> --palette --posterize K --to <folder>`. An experimental "Perceived colours" switch shows the colours as the eye sees them, adapted to the surrounding light (`--perceived` on the command line). Settings: `[gui.palette_view]`, `[palette] perceived_*`.
- **Changed**: the large histogram opens with `g i` (was `Shift+I`), like `g p` for the large palette.
