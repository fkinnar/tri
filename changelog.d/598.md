- **Added**: light mode for network folders (NAS, iCloud): only thumbnails are computed in the background. The preview of the shown image and its neighbours comes when you look at them, an image opened in full screen gets its whole cache, and analyses wait for "Compute all" (one folder or one selection at a time). On by default for network places; right-click on a folder to turn it on or off, and a feather marks where it starts (`[light]`).
- **Fixed**: switching to another folder on a slow disk (NAS) is faster: the folder left behind stops computing thumbnails, reading its ratings and watching for changes while the new one opens, instead of competing with it for the disk.
