- **Added**: offloading cuts a card into sessions anywhere, not only by day: cuts proposed at day changes and long pauses in shooting (`[offload] split_days`, `split_pause_minutes`, `max_sessions`), placed or removed by hand in a gallery of the whole card ("Sessions…", with scissors and glue tools as on an editing table, or the `c` key; checked photos can also be grouped into a session of their own, even if they do not follow each other); two sessions on the same day get their start time in their folder name.
- **Added**: "Forget this card" in the offload dialog (and `tri --offload <card> --forget`): the card's images are removed from the offload history, the copies stay.
- **Changed**: the offload gallery is centred in its window; its thumbnails and previews are kept in the cache and follow the copies, so the same card opens faster and the offloaded session shows its thumbnails at once.
