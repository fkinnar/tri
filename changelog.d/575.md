- **Watched folders**: right-click a folder and choose "Watch this folder" (or press `w` in the folder panel); an eye marks it in the tree. tri walks watched folders in the background at launch, after 10 minutes of inactivity, and walks a removable disk when it is plugged in (never a camera card). All photos then knows their photos without opening each folder. A renamed folder, or a photo moved on its own outside tri, is found again with its notes and palettes. Settings are in `[index]`: `roots`, `walk_at_launch`, `walk_plugged`, `walk_idle_minutes`, `walk_depth` and `walk_pause_ms`.
