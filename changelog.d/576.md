- **Search several colours at once**: the colour box takes up to 5 colours, and an image must contain them all. Choose **Absolute** (all colours count the same) or **Hierarchical** (the first colour counts the most, and images where it covers more come first). The box also has swatches: 6 pure colours, 6 "photo" colours (sky, foliage, skin, sunset, sea, earth) and 6 of your own, which you fill from the current colour and which are kept in the configuration. Settings: `[gui.colour_search]` and `[palette] colour_hierarchy`.
