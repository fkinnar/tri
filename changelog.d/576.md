- **Search several colours at once**: the colour box takes up to 5 colours, and an image must contain them all. Choose **Absolute** (all colours count the same) or **Hierarchical**: the images where the first colour covers the most come first, then the second colour breaks ties. The 5 search colours are swatches: click "+" to store the current colour, click a colour to adjust it with the wheel, right-click to clear it. The box also has swatches: 6 pure colours, 6 "photo" colours (sky, foliage, skin, sunset, sea, earth) and 6 of your own, kept in the configuration. A new **Proximity** sort orders the results of colour, palette and similar-image searches from the closest; the searches themselves only filter, so you can also keep them sorted by name or date. Starting a search switches to Proximity, and stopping it brings back the previous sort. From the command line: `tri <folder> --palette --colours "#d0a080,#6a7a80" [--hierarchical]`. Settings: `[gui.colour_search]`, `[palette] colour_hierarchy` and `colour_proximity`.
